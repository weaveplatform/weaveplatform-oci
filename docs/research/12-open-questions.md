it. Owner types: **counsel** (legal), **GitHub support** (needs an answer from GitHub), **benchmark**
(measure on real hardware), **spike** (a short implementation experiment), **product** (a weave
decision). "Blocks" names the decision record whose Constraints section carries the question.
